Synopsis Self-proclaimed tech visionary Matthew Wiseman and his content-creator accomplice, Casey, film everything without permission as they attempt to pull off the greatest fraud of all time.

Brazenly lying about a product that doesn’t exist to investors terrified of missing out, journalists desperate to discover the next genius, influencers eager to dress sponsorship up as belief, and staff certain somebody else must have checked.

The more successful the lie becomes, the more reality it requires. Forcing them into the irritating business of employing real people, placating regulators and maintaining the appearance of a functioning company.

And then one of the jobsworth engineers makes the product real, leaving Matthew with an even more irritating problem: legitimacy.		 The Magician follows charismatic, yet volatile hitman Ray Shoesmith around Melbourne while he is being filmed by his neighbour and film student Massimo. As he goes about his chilling business — threatening violence and in some cases making good on those threats — the camera continues to roll. Fuelled by offbeat conversation, dark humour and emotional crises - the character is a killer, a man who would as soon pat the dog as shoot it, but without smoothing out any of the rough edges. Under contract to kill a low life of the Melbourne underworld he receives a more tempting offer. The activities of rampaging, indiscriminate serial killer Ben are recorded by a willingly complicit documentary team, who eventually become his accomplices and active participants. Ben provides casual commentary on the nature of his work and arbitrary musings on topics of interest to him, such as music or the conditions of low-income housing, and even goes so far as to introduce the documentary crew to his family. But their reckless indulgences soon get the better of them. Flatmates Viago, Deacon, and Vladislav are three vampires who are just trying to get by in modern society; from paying rent and sticking to the housework roster to trying to get invited in to nightclubs, they’re just like anyone else – except they’re immortal and must feast on human blood. When their 8,000 year old flatmate, Petyr, turns 20-something human hipster, Nick, into a vampire, the guys must teach him the ropes and guide him through his new-found eternal life. In return they are forced to learn about modern society, fashions, and technology. But it’s the introduction of Nick’s human friend, Stu, that really changes the vampire’s lives and attitudes towards the ever-changing world around them. When Stu’s life is threatened, the vamps show us that even though your heart may be cold and dead, it doesn’t mean you have no feelings. FOUR LIONS tells an authentic and startlingly funny story of four wannabe jihadis caught in a spiral of ideology, squabbles and lethal errors. Omar wants to “bring havoc to this torture endorsing Disneyland”, Barry wants to bring on Armageddon by bombing a mosque. Sent home in disgrace from training camp, Omar takes on the mission himself so Barry does everything in his power to trip him up. In a hilarious and breathtaking climax Omar finds himself hurtling towards the London Marathon in a van full of explosives, fancy dress costumes and a cell of berks who all want to strike a blow for the cause but can’t strike a match without punching themselves in the face.
